# Privacy

Mochi is a desktop app. When you connect a Google account, you sign in through a Google app you own, made in your own Google Cloud project.

- Your Google data goes from Google to your computer only. Mochi's makers run no server that sees it.
- Mochi keeps your Google sign-in on your computer, so it can act for you when you ask.
- When you ask an agent to use Gmail, Calendar or Drive, what it reads goes to the AI model you chose (such as Claude or Codex) under that provider's own terms.
- Mochi sells nothing and shares nothing with anyone else.
- To disconnect, remove the account in Mochi, or revoke the app at your Google account's security settings.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
